# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Older adults considering an everyday companion and family members supporting someone they love. The landing is English-only; the current pilot focuses on the Bay Area.

## Product Purpose

Explain Lar, conversation and everyday context; help visitors understand how LaresCare works and submit a pilot enquiry for themselves or a loved one. The primary action leads to the Home pilot form.

## Brand Personality

Familiar, considerate, calm. Preserve the approved LaresCare Figma identity and existing Manrope, coral, navy and cream design system.

## Anti-references

Avoid presenting the service as medical or emergency care. Avoid adding unapproved product claims or replacing the approved Lar identity with a different character. This is a focused refinement of the existing design, not a new visual direction.

## Design Principles

- Keep the person's independence and perspective central.
- Explain family sharing as permission-based.
- Use conversation and real-life context to explain the product.
- Preserve clear hierarchy and readable text across desktop and mobile.
- Retain current URLs and the existing enquiry integration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, native form controls and reduced-motion support. The agreed white CTA text remains a constraint while gradient alternatives are explored.
